## 📸 How It Works

### 1. Upload

Click **"Tap to Upload"** and select your photo.

### 2. Frame

Your image is automatically placed inside the HH GOA 2026 circular frame.

### 3. Preview

See your finished profile picture with the sunset-inspired design.

### 4. Download

Click **Download** to save your PNG.

### 5. Share

Share your creation with the Goa community using:

`#FrameInGoa`
